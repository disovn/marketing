```text

→ creator tự đăng
```

# 6. New Operational Workflow

Recommended managed workflow:

```text
Campaign
→ invite/assign creator
→ creator accept
→ drafting
→ submission
→ review/revision
→ approval
→ publish
→ payout
→ completion
```

* operational visibility
* response timing
* deadline tracking
* revision tracking
* SLA visibility
* operational intelligence data
