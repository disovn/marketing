# Brainstorming Summary — Creator Operations Infrastructure

## 1. Current Situation

Current product includes:
- Campaign management
- Creator registration/application
- Reward calculation based on campaign rules
- Payout management
- White-label platform for clients
- CRM-like creator management capabilities
- Video crawling and campaign tracking

Current business model:
- Started as a custom MVP for one client
- Dedicated development/operation team working monthly
- Client currently perceives the company partly as a development vendor
- Client has enterprise relationships and creator access
- Product is now becoming operationally critical

Key insight:
The company is evolving from:
- custom dev vendor
into:
- creator operations infrastructure provider.

---

# 2. Strategic Positioning

## What NOT to position as
Avoid positioning as:
- creator CRM
- affiliate platform
- campaign management tool
- creator dashboard software

Those are increasingly commodity layers.

---

## Recommended positioning
Position as:

### Creator Operations Infrastructure
or
### AI-powered Creator Operations Platform
or
### Creator Commerce Operating System

Focus on:
- moderation
- compliance
- creator trust
- operational workflow
- payout integrity
- AI-assisted operation
- workforce orchestration
- campaign execution quality

---

# 3. Current Moat Analysis

These are necessary but not defensible long-term:
- CRM
- campaign management
- creator registration
- payout UI
- dashboards
- basic reward rules

Current/future signals:
- creator score
- trust score
- compliance history
- performance consistency
- payout reliability
- category affinity
- spam probability
- moderation history

This is a strategic moat.

### 3. Workforce Orchestration
Current strength:
- assignment
- review queues
- operational coordination
- workload management

Future value:
- SLA optimization
- reviewer scoring
- auto-routing
- QA escalation
- intelligent assignment

This is highly underrated and difficult to replace.

### 4. Operational Data Flywheel
Every moderation action improves:
- AI moderation

- routing intelligence

The system gains value as operational volume increases.

# 4. Strategic Architecture

## Recommended Architecture Layers

### Layer 1 — White-label Client Layer
Client-facing features:
- campaign management
- creator management
- payout management
- reporting
- branding
- permissions
- configurable workflow

This is the interface layer.

---

### Layer 2 — Shared Core Platform
Shared reusable platform:
- moderation workflow
- operation engine
- creator lifecycle
- payout workflow
- event system
- audit trail
- SLA management

This should remain centralized.

---

### Layer 3 — Intelligence Layer
Proprietary layer:
- creator scoring
- moderation AI
- recommendation
- fraud detection
- benchmark engine
- optimization logic
- routing intelligence

This is the real moat.

---

# 5. CRM Strategy

## Important Principle
Do NOT build:
- custom CRM per client.

Build:
- configurable creator operation framework.

---

## What should be configurable
Allowed customization:
- creator lifecycle stages
- tags
- commission rules
- approval workflow
- campaign templates
- permission models
- UI configuration
- reporting views

---

## What should remain proprietary
Keep centralized:
- creator scoring logic
- moderation models
- fraud rules
- recommendation engines
- network benchmark
- operational intelligence
- AI routing

Expose only outputs, not underlying logic.

---

# 6. Data Ownership Strategy

## Client-owned Data
Clients should own:
- creator profiles
- campaigns
- payout data
- uploaded content
- CRM notes
- contracts
- raw operational records

Allow:
- export
- migration
- API access

This improves enterprise trust.

---

## Platform-owned Intelligence
The platform should own:
- scoring models
- moderation logic
- fraud detection
- recommendation systems
- benchmark models
- optimization logic

Clients can consume:
- scores
- insights
- recommendations

But should not own:
- model internals
- training systems
- cross-client intelligence.

---

## Cross-network Data
Cross-client intelligence should remain aggregated/anonymized:
- percentile rankings
- category benchmark
- quality benchmark
- approval trends

Avoid exposing raw network data.

---

# 7. Operational Positioning

## Current evolution
The company is evolving from:
- software vendor
into:
- operational dependency.

This is important because:
- operation workflow now depends on the platform
- moderation depends on the platform
- payout validation depends on the platform
- operational efficiency depends on the platform

The platform is becoming the execution layer.

---

# 8. Recommended Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Strengthen Operational Core
Focus:
- moderation
- operational efficiency
- SLA
- workforce orchestration
- creator trust scoring
- compliance

- advanced recommendation yet

- autonomous moderation routing
- compliance automation

Goal:
AI-assisted operations infrastructure.

# 10. Biggest Risks

## Risk 1 — Becoming a Feature Factory
Danger:
- endless custom requests
- client-specific workflow explosion
- low scalability

Mitigation:
- configurable core
- reusable architecture
- charge heavily for non-reusable customizations

---

## Risk 2 — Remaining Perceived as Dev Vendor
Danger:
- price pressure
- replaceability
- no strategic leverage

Mitigation:
- emphasize intelligence
- emphasize operational optimization
- emphasize moderation and compliance
- emphasize workflow expertise

---

## Risk 3 — Embedding Intelligence Into Client Codebases
Danger:
- impossible centralized improvement
- fragmented systems
- weak moat

Mitigation:
- shared intelligence services
- centralized APIs
- platform-owned operational intelligence

---

The strongest direction appears to be:

## Creator Operations Infrastructure
with:
- AI moderation

- creator intelligence
